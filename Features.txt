ChaoticFiles 0.1 is a Qt 6 / QML file manager for Linux. The engine is plain C++20 and does not depend on Qt.

Browsing
- Folder list with name, date modified, type and size.
- Open folders with a double click or Enter. Open files in their default app.
- Back, forward, up and refresh, with buttons, Alt+Left/Right/Up, F5 and the side buttons of a mouse.
- Sort by any column; folders stay first. Natural order: file2 comes before file10.
- Hidden files on or off with Ctrl+H.
- Select with click, Ctrl+click, Shift+click, the arrow keys, Ctrl+A, and by typing a name.
- A folder refreshes by itself when something in it changes.
- Clear messages for folders you can't open, missing folders and broken links.
- Folders are read on a background thread, so a huge or slow folder does not freeze the window.

Tabs
- Windows 11 style tabs in the title bar.
- New tab Ctrl+T, close Ctrl+W or middle click, switch with Ctrl+Tab, reopen with Ctrl+Shift+T.
- Drag a tab to reorder. Tabs shrink as more open, then the strip scrolls with the wheel.
- Each tab has its own folder, history, sorting, selection and scroll position.
- Tabs come back at the next start.
- Middle click a folder, or right click it, to open it in a new tab.

Address bar
- Every folder in the path can be clicked.
- The arrow after a folder lists its subfolders.
- Click the empty part or press Ctrl+L to type a path. ~ works, and folders are suggested as you type.
- Long paths collapse into a "…" button.

Sidebar and status bar
- Home, the standard user folders, the file system, and mounted drives, which update by themselves.
- Item count, selection count and size, free disk space.

Interface
- Dark, light and system themes. System follows the desktop's dark or light preference.
- Draws its own title bar. Window buttons are off by default on tiling desktops, and can be switched on.
- Right click menus on files and on empty space. Copy Path.
